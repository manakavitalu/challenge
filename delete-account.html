Data Safety & Deletion Policy Details

What Data Is Permanently Deleted:

  • Account Credentials: Google Auth link and unique user identifier (UUID).
  • User Profile: Display name, profile photo URL, and public player statistics.
  • Game Progress: Total points earned, completed crossword count, total solve duration.
  • Streaks & Rankings: Current daily streak, longest streak, and weekly champion entries.
  • Social Connections: Friend list, friend code, and pending friend requests.
  • Saved Game State: Current in-progress crossword puzzle boards.

Data Retention & Timelines:

  • Data Retention: Zero data retained. All records are permanently purged from Supabase cloud database servers.
  • Instant Processing: In-App deletions deletions take effect immediately.
  • Web Form Processing: Requests submitted through the form are reviewed and purged within 24 to 48 hours.
